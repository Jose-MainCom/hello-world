# 👋 About me
I'm a software engineer driven by genuine curiosity about how things work under the hood. Over the years I've moved across different tech stacks — never married to any single one — picking up whatever the problem at hand demanded. That said, my strongest muscle memory lives in C#/.NET, Node.js, and the Azure ecosystem.

What I care about most is being pragmatic: delivering solutions that actually meet business requirements without cutting corners on performance, security, or scalability. Clean, maintainable code isn't a luxury — it's how you keep shipping reliably over time.

I try to stay close to where the industry is heading — whether that's infrastructure as code, DevOps practices, or new patterns in distributed systems — not to chase hype, but because staying current is what lets me adapt quickly to whatever team or challenge comes next.
